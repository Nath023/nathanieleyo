date: 2026-10-10

I've been thinking about what it means to build something and share the process along the way.

A lot of good work happens quietly. Ideas take shape, projects run into problems, and lessons emerge through trial and error.

Writing about the process gives those lessons a place to live. It helps me organise my thoughts, track my progress, and connect with people working on similar problems.

This blog is a place to document what I'm learning about technology, business, and building useful things.

I don't expect every post to have a perfect answer. I want to record the questions, experiments, and decisions along the way.
